# Spendigo SmartCart - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** December 16, 2025

## 1. Compliance Statement
Spendigo Inc. is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)** and Quebec's **Act Respecting the Protection of Personal Information in the Private Sector (Law 25)**.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Identity:** Name, Email, Phone Number (for order updates).
- **Financial:** Last 4 digits of card (tokenized via Stripe). We do NOT store full card numbers.
- **Location:** Geolocation (with consent) for "SmartCart" optimization.
- **Purchase History:** To provide "Recall Alerts" and historical receipts.

## 3. Data Residency
All Critical Personal Information (PII) is stored in **Canada** or **United States** data centers with strict "Data Processing Addendums" in place ensuring equivalency of protection.

## 4. Quebec Residents (Law 25 Specifications)
- **Data Protection Officer:** [Name to be appointed], legal@spendigo.ca
- **Right to Erasure:** You may request deletion of your account via the App Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. This is irreversible.
- **Cross-Border Transfer:** We disclose that data may be processed by Stripe (USA) for payment facilitation.

## 5. Data Retention
- Transaction records are kept for **7 years** as required by Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) for tax audit purposes.
- Inactive accounts are anonymized after 2 years of inactivity.
