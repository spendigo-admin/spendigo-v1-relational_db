# Spendigo SmartCart - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 11, 2026

## 1. Compliance Statement
Spendigo Inc. ("Spendigo", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)** and Quebec's **Act Respecting the Protection of Personal Information in the Private Sector (Law 25)**.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect information to provide and improve our services:
- **Identity Information:** Name, Email, Phone Number (used for account verification and order updates).
- **Financial Information:** Last 4 digits of credit/debit cards and expiry dates. All payment processing is handled via **Stripe**; determining that we do **NOT** store full card numbers on our servers.
- **Location Data:** Geolocation (with exclusive user consent) to power "SmartCart" optimization, finding nearby stores, and estimating delivery times.
- **Usage & Purchase History:** Order details and browsing history to provide "Recall Alerts", digital receipts, and personalized recommendations.
- **Device Information:** IP address, device type, and operating system for security and fraud prevention.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- To facilitate transactions between you and Independent Sellers.
- To optimize your shopping cart using our "SmartCart" algorithm.
- To communicate with you regarding your orders, account security, and updates.
- To comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax audits).

## 4. Data Residency & Security
All Critical Personal Information (PII) is encrypted and stored in **Canada** or **United States** data centers. We maintain strict **Data Processing Addendums (DPAs)** with all third-party vendors to ensure they provide a level of protection equivalent to Canadian standards.

## 5. Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal data. We share data only as necessary to fulfill services:
- **Merchants:** Your name and order details are shared with the specific Independent Store fulfilling your order.
- **Service Providers:** Payment processors (Stripe), email services (for notifications), and hosting providers.
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities.

## 6. Quebec Residents (Law 25 Specifications)
In compliance with Law 25, we provide the following specific rights and disclosures:
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** The designated Privacy Officer can be reached at legal@spendigo.ca.
- **Right to Erasure:** You may request the deletion of your account and associated data via **App Settings > Privacy > Delete Account**. Note that this action is irreversible.
- **Cross-Border Transfer:** We disclose that necessary data may be processed by our payment partner, Stripe, in the United States.
- **Profiling:** If we use automated processing to make significant decisions about you, you have the right to be informed.

## 7. Data Retention
- **Transaction Records:** Retained for **7 years** as required by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) for tax audit purposes.
- **Inactive Accounts:** Account profile data is anonymized after **2 years** of continuous inactivity.
- **Marketing Data:** You can opt-out of marketing communications at any time, after which your contact details are removed from marketing lists immediately.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking
We use cookies to maintain your session, remember preferences, and analyze platform performance. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.
