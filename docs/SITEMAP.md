# Spendigo Site Map

**Last Updated**: 2026-01-12
**Status**: Beta (Routes Implemented)
**Base URL**: `https://spendigo.ca`

---

## 🛍️ Consumer App (Public)

### Core Navigation
- [Home](https://spendigo.ca) - Store listings, hero banner, featured stats.
- [Search](https://spendigo.ca/search) - Global product search filtered by category/store.
- [SmartCart](https://spendigo.ca/smartcart) - Wishlist optimization engine (Cheapest Price Finder).
- [How It Works](https://spendigo.ca/how-it-works) - Explainer page for the Optimizer.
- [Shopping Cart](https://spendigo.ca/cart) - Manage items and quantities.
- [Checkout](https://spendigo.ca/checkout) - Payment, delivery address, and order placement.
- [Surveys](https://spendigo.ca/surveys) - **New**: Participate in polls and provide feedback.

### Store & Product (Examples)
- [Store Detail](https://spendigo.ca/store/1) - Products, flyers, deals, and ratings.
- [Product Detail](https://spendigo.ca/product/p1) - Product info, images, and add-to-cart.

### User Account
- [Profile](https://spendigo.ca/profile) - User settings, saved addresses, order history.
- [Notifications](https://spendigo.ca/notifications) - Price drop alerts and order updates.
- [Order Tracking](https://spendigo.ca/order/101) - Visual timeline of active orders.

## 👔 Merchant Admin (Secure)

### Dashboard
- [Merchant Dashboard](https://spendigo.ca/merchant/dashboard) - **Overview**: Sales stats, recent orders, quick actions.

### Management
- [Product Catalog](https://spendigo.ca/merchant/products) - Add/Edit/Delete products, manage stock.
- [Order Manager](https://spendigo.ca/merchant/orders) - View incoming orders, update status (Preparing/Ready).
- [Analytics](https://spendigo.ca/merchant/analytics) - **New**: Detailed sales reports and traffic insights.
- [Flyers](https://spendigo.ca/merchant/flyers) - Create and manage weekly digital flyers.
- [Deals](https://spendigo.ca/merchant/deals) - Create one-day offers and clearance sales.
- [Sponsored Listings](https://spendigo.ca/merchant/sponsored) - **New**: Promote products to top of listings.
- [Settings](https://spendigo.ca/merchant/settings) - Store profile and configuration.
- [Subscription](https://spendigo.ca/merchant/subscription) - **New**: Manage platform subscription tier.

## 🛡️ System Admin (Internal)

### Dashboard & Monitoring
- [System Dashboard](https://spendigo.ca/admin/dashboard) - **Overview**: Platform health, revenue stats, system alerts.
- [Audit Logs](https://spendigo.ca/admin/audit-logs) - **Security**: View system access logs and critical actions.

### Entity Management
- [User Management](https://spendigo.ca/admin/users) - **Users**: View/Ban consumers and merchants.
- [Store Management](https://spendigo.ca/admin/stores) - **Stores**: Approve new merchants, oversee compliance.
- [Master Catalog](https://spendigo.ca/admin/catalog) - **Catalog**: Manage global product definitions (Master Products).
- [Pending Approvals](https://spendigo.ca/admin/catalog/pending) - **Verification**: Queue for Merchant Product Requests.

### Platform Tools
- [Ad Campaigns](https://spendigo.ca/admin/ads) - **Ads**: Manage carousel campaigns and sponsorships.
- [Survey Manager](https://spendigo.ca/admin/surveys) - **Surveys**: Create polls and view responses.
- [System Tools](https://spendigo.ca/admin/tools) - **DevOps**: Maintenance mode, data migration tools.
- [Seed Users](https://spendigo.ca/admin/seed) - **Dev**: Initialize test data.
- [Settings](https://spendigo.ca/admin/settings) - **Config**: Global platform settings.
